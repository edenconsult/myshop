Open the register and make a sale

  1. Open Point of Sale in the navigation. You can also press F2 from another page.
  2. Search for a product, then select its card to add it to the customer cart.
  3. Check the cart and quantities. Remove an item or clear the cart if needed.
  4. Select Complete Sale (or Pay on the compact register view) to record the sale. The completed sale displays its receipt.

Find and add products

  • Use the register search field to search by product name.
  • Select a product card to add one to the cart. Use the quantity controls to adjust the amount, up to the available stock.
  • If no products match, clear the search to browse the available catalog.
  • Barcode scanners are not integrated; use the on-screen product search.

If a product you need is missing, ask a store administrator to check its availability in the sale catalog.

Sales reports and receipts

For individual transactions, open Orders to inspect receipts and download a receipt PDF.

{% else %}

Troubleshooting

No products in stock match this search
Clear the search to browse the available catalog. If the item is still missing, ask an administrator to check its availability.
Need to find the register quickly?
Press F2 from another page. On the register, press / to focus product search when you are not already typing in a field.
