# 02. The owner's hub

## What this is for

This is the owner's control panel and the shop's front room: today's takings, today's receipt count,
what has run out, who sold what, and a button for each of the four workflows the shop is built around —
the register, the product ledger, past receipts and the reports. It is not a button for every other
screen in the building. Two owner screens have no shortcut here at all: the accounts roster lives in the
account menu at the top right, and the delivery receipts open from the ledger page. Nothing is edited
here. It is the place you stand to decide where to go next, the place you return to when you lose your
way inside the shop, and the place a new owner learns the shape of the whole system in one screen.

## You need

- **Who can open it:** the owner account only. A cashier who reaches this address is not refused — they
  are shown the till instead, at the same address, because the screen decides what to draw by whose
  account is looking at it.
- **Where it lives:** it is your home screen. After signing in you are already standing on it.
- **How to get back to it from anywhere in the shop:** press the Noble Cosmetics mark at the left end of
  the header, or the `Central Hub` item beside it. On a phone, open the drawer from the menu button and
  take `Central Hub`. From a password-change confirmation, the `Continue` button lands here too.
- **How to get here from where you are now:** if you have just finished chapter 01, you are already
  here. If you are on any other owner screen, the header mark is one press away.
- **What must already exist:** accounts and a product list. With no sales today every money tile reads
  zero and the transaction table says `No customer transactions yet.` with a `Start a sale` link inside
  it — the screen still works, it is just quiet.
- **The figures in the frames below** are the shop's own for the day they were photographed. Yours will
  differ; the layout will not.

The four workflow cards are the screen's purpose: they are the only four destinations the hub treats as
first-class. The welcome banner's `Current Till:` figure is today's takings for the whole shop, not for
you, and it is the same number that appears again on the reports card as `Today Gross` and again in the
snapshot as today's total sales.

### 2. Scroll to the snapshot and the recent receipts

Still the same frame, further down: `Today's Store Snapshot` with the line `Essential metrics for Store
#01 today` and a `Live update` dot beside it, then four tiles — today's total sales with a comparison to
yesterday, completed orders with `Today at Store #01` under it, products needing restock with a couple of
sample names and an `Order Stock Now` link, and sales by cashier. Below that, `Recent Customer
Transactions` with the columns `Receipt #`, `Purchased Items`, `Payment`, `Cashier`, `Total`, `Time`, and
on the right the `Quick Store Tools` panel holding `Open Register`, `Sales Reports`, `Help & Support` and
the tip `Press F2 from another page to open the register`.

### 3. Press F2 from anywhere else to reach the register

Nothing to photograph for this one: on any screen of the shop, F2 jumps you to the register. It is the
same jump as the header's `Point of Sale` item, and it does nothing when you are already standing on the
register. The tip on the hub is aimed at cashiers, but the key works for an owner as well.

### 4. Open the same hub on a phone

![The same owner dashboard squeezed into a phone column. The header is reduced to the Noble Cosmetics mark, the account badge, a dark-mode button and a menu button. Nothing is hidden on a phone — the same buttons are all present, just one per row, and the restock
sample line has room to name a third product. The menu button at the top right opens the drawer that
holds `Central Hub`, `Point of Sale`, `Products & Stock`, `Orders`, `Sales Reports`, `Help & Support`,
and at its foot your name, your role written out, and the red `Log Out`.

### 5. Use the cards to reach the screen you actually want

Again, no new frame: each card button opens an ordinary screen covered by its own chapter. From this hub,
`Launch Checkout Register` and `+ New Customer Sale (POS)` both open the till. `View Product Catalog`
opens the ledger, `+ Add New Product` and `Add Product` open the same ledger with the Add Product dialog
already standing open on top of it, and `Restock Alerts (108)` opens its Low & Out of Stock tab — three
views of one screen, not three screens. `View All Orders` opens the receipts ledger; `View Daily Report`
opens the reports screen. The `Order Stock Now` link in the restock tile goes to the same tab as
`Restock Alerts (108)`.

### 6. Open the built-in help from the hub

The `Quick Store Tools` panel carries a `Help & Support` shortcut with the line `How to use the register
and store tools` under it, and the header menu carries the same destination as `Help`. Press it and you
get a page headed `Help & Support` with the line `Quick guides for the register, product search, and
store reports.`, four topic pills — `Register`, `Finding products`, `Reports and receipts`,
`Troubleshooting` — and four panels: `Open the register and make a sale`, `Find and add products`,
`Sales reports and receipts`, and `Troubleshooting`. The register panel ends with an `Open Register`
button. Step 7 photographs that page, so the wording above is a description you can check against a real
frame.

This is the page both roles reach once they are signed in. The help screen asks for a sign-in and for a
working account, and an owner and a cashier both qualify, so nobody gets it anonymously and nobody is
turned away from it. Only one part of it changes between the two: where this owner reads the Sales
Reports and Orders paragraphs, a cashier reading the same page gets `Sales reports and the Orders ledger
are available to administrators. Ask a store administrator if you need to review a report or past
receipt.` in their place. The rest of the page — the register steps, the product bullets, the F2 tip — is
word for word the same for both of you.

An unsigned browser never sees any of this. The help page is not a public noticeboard in this build: it
sits behind the same door as everything else, so arriving there signed out gives you the sign-in card
instead of the guide. Sign in first, then press Help.

## When it goes wrong

- **A cashier who types the hub address is not refused.** They get the till, drawn at the same address,
  with no click involved: the screen looks at whose account is asking and picks the register instead of
  the dashboard. If a cashier says "my dashboard is different from yours", that is correct and not a
  fault.
- **Anyone who is not signed in gets the sign-in card** — including you, after your session ends while
  this page sits open. Pressing a card then brings you back to the door with no note saying why, and
  signing in again puts you on this hub rather than on the screen you had pressed for.
- **`Download CSV` on the reports card downloads nothing.** It navigates to the reports screen, which is
  where `View Daily Report` goes. The real export button is on that screen.
- **`Find Receipt` is a second copy of `View All Orders`.** Both buttons on the orders card open the same
  ledger; there is no separate receipt-search screen behind the magnifier.
- **The restock pill and the restock tile can disagree with the ledger's own low-stock filter.** The hub
  counts products at zero stock; the ledger's low-stock filter counts products at or under each
  product's own warning point. Both are right — they answer different questions.
- **The restock tile lists at most three names.** The sample line is a sample; the big count beside it is
  the real zero-stock total. `Order Stock Now` does not open that list: it opens the ledger's Low & Out of
  Stock tab, which counts more products than the tile does, for the reason in the bullet above.
- **A day with no sales yesterday shows `No sales yesterday to compare`** under the total-sales tile
  instead of a percentage. That is the tile working, not a missing figure.
- **The recent-transaction table is not filtered to today.** It shows the latest few receipts the shop
  has, so a sale from yesterday or last week can sit in it while `Completed Orders` above counts today
  only. The relative times in the last column are the hint.
- **The Help icon is missing from the shop's icon set,** so the glyph never appears where it is asked
  for: the hub card shows a blank white square where it should sit, and the desktop header shows an empty
  gap before the word `Help` — you can see that gap in the help frame in step 7 and in chapter 01's
  profile frame. The phone drawer asks for the same missing glyph, but no frame in this manual shows the
  drawer open, so that one is from the code rather than from a picture. The link itself works everywhere.
- **The help page tells a cashier something different from an owner.** For a cashier its reports panel
  reads `Sales reports and the Orders ledger are available to administrators. Ask a store administrator
  if you need to review a report or past receipt.` That is accurate, and it is the only place the shop
  writes the owner-only rule out in words instead of just refusing the screen. Other lines do ask a
  cashier to go to an administrator, but about something else: twice on the help page about whether a
  missing product is available, and on the accounts screen about roles — `New accounts sign in as
  salespersons; roles are not chosen here.` Neither of those is a permissions notice.
- **A card press that seems to do nothing** is nearly always the session ending underneath you. Every
  card and every shortcut on this hub is a plain link, so a press always takes the whole window with it
  and the whole window comes back as the sign-in card; that is the only shape this screen can make. The
  other shape — a login box appearing in the middle of a page you were already using — belongs to the
  screens that load their own tables, like the ledger, not to the hub. Chapter 01 describes it.

## Notes & limits

- **This screen changes nothing.** No stock, no price, no order, no account. Every tile is a read, and
  every button is a lift to somewhere that does the work.
- **`Live update` is a label, not a behaviour.** The snapshot does not refresh itself while you watch it;
  it is a photograph of the moment the page was opened. Reload to see a sale someone else just rung up.
- **`Ready for Checkout` is decoration.** It is a fixed pill on the point-of-sale card, not a status the
  shop measures.
- **Two card lines are not kept by the code, and between them they break three promises.** `Ring up
  orders, scan barcodes, and accept Cash, MoMo, or Card payments without delay` — barcode scanners are not
  integrated, and a sale records only an amount, with nowhere to say whether it came in as cash, mobile
  money or card. `Track customer purchases, search past receipts, and manage refunds or reprints` —
  reprints exist, refunds do not. `Fast Barcode Lookup` and `Instant Receipts` on the point-of-sale card
  are those same two broken promises in short form, not two more gaps. `Excel / CSV Export` is kept, but
  only after you reach the reports screen.
- **Everything money-shaped here is today only.** There is no date picker on the hub and no way to look
  at yesterday without moving to the reports screen.
- **`Sales by Cashier` lists at most three people**, and anyone with no names filled in on their profile
  appears as their login name.
- **The hub cannot tell you who is signed in elsewhere**, whether the background service is alive, or
  whether the last backup ran. Those are upkeep questions for chapter 13.
- **There is no search box and no filter on this screen.** Finding one sale or one product is done on the
  screen that owns it.
- **What only the owner sees:** this whole dashboard, and every destination behind the cards except the
  register. The accounts, deliveries, stock counts, ledger and reports are owner-only, and a cashier who
  opens one of them is bounced off it without a word: the shop drops them straight back on their own till
  and never shows them the page or the door. The sign-in card is not part of that — it only appears when
  the browser is genuinely signed out. So when a cashier presses something and lands back on their own
  screen, that was a permissions answer, not a crash.
- **What a cashier sees instead at the same address:** the till, with its own takings figure labelled for
  that person rather than for the shop. Chapter 08 covers it.
